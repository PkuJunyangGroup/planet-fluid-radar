Selection & dates

Papers published or updated in the last two Beijing calendar days take priority. When no relevant recent paper is found, a small number of highly relevant papers published within the past year may be added. Topics and content tags are filters, not a request to collect every matching paper. Topic relevance is a rule-based reading aid, not a quality score.

“Published” is the earliest verified publication date, including the first arXiv posting. “Added” is the first time the site recorded the paper. Version updates are tracked separately. Month-only dates retain their original precision. Journal and preprint versions are linked by DOI, explicit publisher relations, or conservative title-and-author matching.

Institution links reflect paper affiliations, not current employment. Universities and research organizations are counted once per paper; departments appear inside their parent institution. Verified ORCID links come from paper or publisher metadata.

Recommendations

Click ☆ to recommend a paper to other readers, and ★ to withdraw your recommendation. Counts are shared across visitors. One anonymous browser identifier is stored on this device; clearing browser storage or changing devices creates a new identity. Stars are informal reader recommendations and do not identify individual teachers or reviewers.

Visitor statistics & privacy

The footer and map show aggregate page views and approximate city locations. IP-based location may reflect a campus gateway or VPN. Only your own IP is returned to your browser; other visitors’ IPs are never displayed. Raw visitor IP records are retained for up to seven Beijing calendar days, while aggregate counts are kept. Each IP is counted once as a visitor per day, with a maximum of 100 page views per day. Visitor-days sum these daily visitor counts and may include repeat visitors across days.

Recommendations use a separate anonymous browser identifier and paper ID; they do not require names, email addresses or IP records. The service runs on Cloudflare. If it is unavailable, paper browsing remains available.
